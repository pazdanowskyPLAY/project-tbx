Welcome to our main page of TBX Project, the text-based sci-fi game!

The year is 2015, late June. During a severe drought, a meteorite falls on Clearwater River Park in Saskatchewan, Canada. The resulting fire, fuelled by favourable weather conditions, slowly spreads towards Fort McMurray in Alberta. The population evacuates from the threatened areas. Those who remain, already cut off from the world, wait patiently for hell on earth to come.
Ewan Mullenix must reach a former military outpost east of Fort McMurray and discover his mother's past for the last time... before fire consumes him alive.
